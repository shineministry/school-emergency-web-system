🔔 How alerts work

In an emergency you get a red high-priority notification from your school with sound and vibration — even with the app closed. Tap I AM SAFE right in the notification, or open the app for the full-screen alert.

🛡️ After an alert

Follow staff instructions and leave via the nearest safe exit. Tap I AM SAFE so the school knows you are accounted for.

📲 Install this app

Use your browser menu → Install app / Add to home screen so alerts reach you like a native app.
