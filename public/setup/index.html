Set up emergency alerts

School Emergency – alert app for students & staff

Follow the steps for your phone. Setup takes about 2 minutes, once. After that you are always registered — you will get school emergency alerts on your screen anywhere, as long as you have internet.

📱 iPhone FREE

  1. Open this page in Safari (the iPhone's blue compass app).
  2. Tap Share (square with arrow ↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open the new School Emergency icon on your home screen.
  4. Sign in with your school account.
  5. Tap Enable notifications → Allow.
  6. Done. Emergency messages now appear on your lock screen with sound.

🤖 Android FULL-SCREEN ALARM

  1. Download the app: tap the button below.
  2. If Android warns "File might be harmful" / "unsafe app" → choose Download anyway / Install anyway. It is the school's own app, not from the Play Store.
  3. Open the downloaded file → Install → Open.
  4. Sign in with your school account (server address is already filled in).
  5. Tap START MONITORING → allow notifications and battery optimization prompts.
  6. Done. Emergencies open a full-screen red alarm with siren — even when your phone is on silent.

In an emergency: your phone shows the school's red alert with instructions. Tap OK – I AM SAFE so the school knows you are accounted for — you can do this directly from the notification.

Sign in once — you stay signed in on that phone. Alerts only arrive after you sign in and enable notifications.
